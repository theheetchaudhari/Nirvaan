# 🌍 NIRVAAN — Carbon Footprint Tracker

> Net Impact Reduction Via Action & Analytics for Nature

A modern and interactive carbon footprint tracking web application that helps users understand how their daily lifestyle impacts the environment through simple calculations, visual insights, and sustainability awareness.

---

## 🚀 Overview

NIRVAAN is a lightweight sustainability-focused platform designed to calculate and visualize individual carbon emissions from daily activities such as:

- Transportation
- Electricity usage
- Food habits
- Lifestyle choices

The goal is to make climate awareness simple, interactive, and accessible for everyone.

Unlike traditional complex carbon calculators, NIRVAAN focuses on:

- Simplicity
- Modern UI/UX
- Fast interaction
- Real-world awareness
- Beginner-friendly experience
